Landmark Developers states that it began its journey in **2011**.

**2011–2016 — Laying the Foundation**

The company describes this period as its foundation stage, with a stated focus on understanding market dynamics, building relationships, establishing trust, transparency, quality, sustainable growth, and redefining real estate through quality and trust.

**2017–2026 — Shaping the Skyline**

The company describes this period as a high-rise development phase, with a stated focus on high-rise development, smartly planned developments, contemporary design, efficient space utilization, investment value, prime locations, lifestyle excellence, and long-term returns.
